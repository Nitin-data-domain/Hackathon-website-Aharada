WHAT IS AHARADA KAUSHAL KUMBH?

Aharada Kaushal Kumbh is a dynamic AI & Machine Learning innovation sprint designed to bring together aspiring data scientists, ML engineers, and industry-minded innovators.

More than just a hackathon, it is a platform for creativity, problem-solving, and real-world application of artificial intelligence. Participants work on real datasets, build predictive models, and transform ideas into impactful solutions.

This event provides students with an opportunity to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry needs. By applying analytical thinking, machine learning techniques, and data-driven decision making, participants showcase their technical “Kaushal” (skills) on a competitive stage.

At Aharada Kaushal Kumbh, you don’t just build models — you build solutions that shape the future of AI.

THE COMPETITION

Aharada Kaushal Kumbh is an Inter-University AI & ML Competition designed to encourage innovation, collaboration, and practical problem solving among students.

Participants from different universities will compete by presenting data-driven solutions to real-world challenges, demonstrating their technical expertise, creativity, and teamwork.

HACKATHON CHALLENGES

PROBLEM STATEMENTS

Select an official problem statement for your hackathon submission. Build innovative, working solutions to solve real campus and educational challenges.

TRACK 01 Easy

Campus Lost & Found System

Problem:

Students often lose their ID cards, books, bottles, and other items on campus. Finding the owner can be difficult.

Build:

A website where students can post lost or found items with a photo, description, location, and contact details.

Must-Have Features:
  • Report a lost or found item.
  • View and search reported items.
  • Filter by item category.
  • Mark an item as returned.
Judging Criteria:

Working search, easy interface, and successful item matching.

TRACK 02 Easy

Smart Student Planner

Problem:

First-year students sometimes struggle to manage assignments, classes, exams, and deadlines.

Build:

A simple student dashboard to organize academic tasks and daily activities.

Must-Have Features:
  • Add assignments and deadlines.
  • Create a daily timetable.
  • Mark tasks as completed.
  • Show pending and completed tasks.
Judging Criteria:

Task management, useful reminders, and a clear dashboard.

TRACK 03 Easy

Green Campus Challenge

Problem:

Students may not know how to dispose of waste properly or how to reduce waste on campus.

Build:

A website that teaches waste segregation and encourages eco-friendly actions.

Must-Have Features:
  • Quiz on waste segregation.
  • Tips for reducing plastic and paper waste.
  • Points for completing green activities.
  • Leaderboard for participating students.
Judging Criteria:

Quiz functionality, accuracy of information, and engaging design.

TRACK 04 Best Choice

Student Placement Prediction System

Problem Statement:

Colleges want to identify students who may need additional support to prepare for campus placements.

Task:

Analyze student data to estimate placement readiness or predict placement outcomes.

Dataset Columns:
CGPA Attendance Aptitude Score Coding Score Communication Score Internship Experience Placement Status
Expected Output:
  • Placement probability or predicted outcome.
  • Factors affecting placement readiness.
  • List of students who may need extra support.
  • Charts showing placement trends.
Recommended Tools:
Excel Power BI Python Pandas Matplotlib Scikit-learn
TRACK 05 Beginner to Intermediate

Student Dropout Risk Analysis

Problem Statement:

Educational institutions want to identify students who may be at risk of leaving their studies.

Task:

Analyze attendance, academic scores, fee payment status, and previous semester results.

Expected Output:
  • Identify patterns associated with dropout risk.
  • Create a risk-level dashboard.
  • Suggest early support measures for students.
Ethical Note: Use anonymized or synthetic data, and treat results as indicators for support, not as final judgments about students.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Who can participate in this hackathon?

Any college student who is passionate about Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and AI. This is an Inter-University challenge hosted by Aharada Education / (IIMT University), so beginners and experts from all universities are welcome!

Is there any registration fee?

Yes, there is a registration fee of ₹500 per team. You can pay via the official UPI QR code (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) during registration and upload your transaction confirmation screenshot with UTR number.

What is the team variation / academic year diversity rule?

To encourage collaboration across batches, every team must include members from at least two different academic years (e.g., 1st year + 2nd year). Teams where all members belong to the same academic year are not permitted.

What is the required team size?

Each team must consist of 3 to 5 members, including the team leader.

What should we bring to the event?

Bring your college ID card, your laptop, chargers, and any specialized hardware you plan to use for your ML project.

Are we allowed to use AI tools like ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot?

Yes, AI tools are allowed under our AI Policy! However, your core problem-solving logic and the overall originality of the project must be your own.
